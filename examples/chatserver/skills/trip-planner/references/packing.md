# Packing by forecast

Match the list to the forecast you fetched. Do not assume weather you did not
look up.

- **Rain chance at or above half on any day:** a compact umbrella or a rain
  jacket, and shoes that dry quickly.
- **Highs above the high twenties:** light clothing, sun protection and a
  refillable water bottle.
- **Lows in single digits:** a warm layer for mornings and evenings.
- **Mixed days:** layers rather than one heavy coat.

Keep the list to five items or fewer, ordered by the day they are first needed.
